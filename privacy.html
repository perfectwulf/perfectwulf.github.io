Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

PerfectWulf Analytics is a private tool used by a single person, the creator of the PerfectWulf channels, to view statistics about their own videos. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the creator signs in with TikTok, the tool reads only that account's own profile statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the list of its public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts. It does not access any other person's account or private messages, and it does not post, edit or delete anything.

Where the data goes

The data is stored in a database on the creator's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server, sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to track anyone.

Retention and deletion

The data stays on the creator's computer until the creator deletes it. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings under the apps connected to the account, and the local database can be deleted at any time.

Contact

The creator can be reached through github.com/perfectwulf.
